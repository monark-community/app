- 2026-05-01: TOTP recovery codes shown as a codeblock with a copy button. The post-enrollment / post-regeneration display in [totp-section.tsx](<monark/app/services/web/src/app/(authed)/account/totp-section.tsx>) was a 2-column `<ul>` of mono `<li>`s ; replaced with a single `<pre>` codeblock (one code per line, mono, scannable) and a small `<Button variant="ghost" size="icon">` anchored top-right with the lucide `Copy` icon.
