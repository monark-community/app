- 2026-07-25: Kanban — fixed unreliable card drops + flattened the board look ([kanban-board-view.tsx](<monark/app/services/web/src/app/(authed)/kanban/kanban-board-view.tsx>), [board-column.tsx](monark/app/packages/kanban/src/client/ui/board-column.tsx), [board-area.tsx](monark/app/packages/kanban/src/client/ui/board-area.tsx)). Root cause of "can't drop into every other column": `closestCorners` collision detection measured distance to the nearest droppable _corner_, so hovering a sparse/empty column snapped onto a card in the neighbouring column — the drop (and the flickery highlight) landed in the wrong place. Switched to a `pointerWithin` strategy (with a `rectIntersection` fallback for gaps + the keyboard sensor) so the column under the pointer is the exact target. Also reworked the move to resolve entirely **on drop** from stable committed state (no mid-drag card-state mutation, which had its own stale-closure races), and added a highlight-only `onDragOver` that lights up the whole resolved column (over a card or empty space alike) instead of only the raw `isOver` body. Visual: columns are now **flat on the background** — no raised muted card, separated by divider borders with underlined headers and no gaps between them (cards keep their card styling). `tsc` (web + kanban) + `eslint` green ; `kanban-board` screenshot updated.
