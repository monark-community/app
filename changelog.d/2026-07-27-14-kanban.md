- 2026-07-27: Kanban — **long-press to drag on touch**, so mobile users can pan the board without grabbing a card. The board's one `DndContext` swapped its single `PointerSensor` for an input-aware pair ([kanban-board-view.tsx](<monark/app/services/web/src/app/(authed)/kanban/kanban-board-view.tsx>)): a `MouseSensor` (6px activation, unchanged desktop feel) plus a `TouchSensor` with a **250ms delay + 8px tolerance** — a quick swipe scrolls, a held press starts the drag. Paired with dropping `touch-action: none` from the card ([kanban-card.tsx](monark/app/packages/kanban/src/client/ui/kanban-card.tsx)) and the column grip so the browser keeps the pan/scroll gesture until the long-press delay elapses. Fixes accidental card moves when trying to scroll the board on narrow screens. `typecheck` (web + kanban) + `lint` green.
