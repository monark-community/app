- 2026-07-26: DataTable — empty / error states center on the visible container, not the table. They rendered as a `<td colSpan>` inside the table, so when wide columns overflowed and forced horizontal scroll the message centered on the (wider-than-viewport) table width and drifted off-screen. Moved them out of the `<table>` into a sibling in the scroll wrapper ([data-table.tsx](monark/app/services/web/src/components/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx)), pinned with `sticky left-0 w-full` so the block sizes to the visible area and stays centered even while the table scrolls sideways (dropped the now-unused `visibleColumnCount`). Verified with a `table-empty-state` screenshot story (8 columns overflowing a 720px container : message stays centred on the visible box). `tsc` + `eslint` green.
