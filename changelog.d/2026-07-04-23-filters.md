- 2026-07-04: Filters — submenu-per-field + data-type-aware inputs in [`FilterMenu`](monark/app/services/web/src/components/patterns/filter-menu.tsx). Desktop renders the submenu ; mobile keeps the full-screen modal but with a labelled, type-aware section per field.
