- 2026-07-04: Calendar — fix archiving a calendar freezing all mouse interaction. Both the sidebar archive confirm ([calendar-sidebar.tsx](<monark/app/services/web/src/app/(authed)/calendar/calendar-sidebar.tsx>)) and the manage dialog's delete ([calendar-manage-dialog.tsx](<monark/app/services/web/src/app/(authed)/calendar/calendar-manage-dialog.tsx>)) fired the delete from Radix's `onCloseAutoFocus`; the mutation's list-refetch re-render unmounted the dialog mid-close, so Radix never restored the `pointer-events: none` it sets on `<body>` while a modal is open — leaving the whole page unclickable. The delete now runs from the confirm/delete action, closes the dialog, and defers the mutation past the close (`setTimeout(…, 0)`) so the body lock is released first ; the deferred-delete refs + `onCloseAutoFocus` handlers were removed.
