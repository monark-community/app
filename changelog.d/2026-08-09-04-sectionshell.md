- 2026-08-09: `SectionShell` — smoothed out the mobile nav-collapse / detail-bar transition, reported sluggish on a real phone. Two changes : (1) the `<main>` scroll listener now batches its state check to at most once per `requestAnimationFrame` instead of running on every raw `scroll` event (which can fire many times per frame on some browsers/devices) — that main-thread work was competing with the browser's own scroll-compositing, which is what made the collapse feel like it lagged behind the actual scroll gesture rather than tracking it. (2) The collapse transition switched from `duration-200 ease-in-out` to `duration-150 ease-out` — `ease-in-out`'s slow start meant the row visibly took a moment to begin reacting ; `ease-out` starts at full speed (immediate response to the gesture) and only eases at the end. `tsc` + `eslint` + 279 web tests green ; verified the 3-row⇄2-row swap still lands correctly under both a normal scroll and a simulated rapid/fling scroll (many `scrollTo` calls a few ms apart) — real-device perceived smoothness on the phone that reported this is unverifiable from here, so worth a second look there.
