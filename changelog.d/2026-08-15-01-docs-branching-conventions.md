- 2026-08-15: Docs: **reconciled the branching conventions**, which said two different things in two places. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) told contributors to branch `feature/my-feature` and open PRs against `main`, while the parallel-work section added to [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) said `feat/` into `develop` ; both `feat/` and `feature/` were live in the branch list (2 each), and every human PR to date had targeted `main` even though `develop` sits 46 commits ahead of it. Settled on one rule in both docs: branches are `<type>/<slug>` using the conventional-commit types (`feat/`, `fix/`, `docs/`, `test/`, `chore/`, plus `tooling/`), `feat/` not `feature/` ; `develop` is the integration branch that everything lands in by PR, and `main` is the release branch promoted from `develop` by the maintainers. The two docs now cross-reference each other so the rule can't quietly diverge again.
