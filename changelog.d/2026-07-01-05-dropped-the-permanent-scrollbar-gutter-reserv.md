- 2026-07-01: Dropped the permanent scrollbar-gutter reservation. Removed `html { scrollbar-gutter: stable }` from [globals.css](monark/app/services/web/src/app/globals.css) — it reserved horizontal gutter space for the vertical scrollbar on every page (even short ones that never scroll) and diverged from stock shadcn, which leaves overlay scroll-locking to Radix's `react-remove-scroll` (it compensates for the scrollbar width itself when a modal opens). The unrelated `body { overflow-x: clip }` bleed-element safety net stays. Trade-off : centered content can shift a few pixels between a tall (scrollbar) and short (no scrollbar) route, the behavior the reservation had masked ; we accept it to match shadcn and avoid the gutter's side effects.
