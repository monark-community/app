# Calendar

Plan meetings, deadlines and releases on your organization's shared, color-coded calendars. Open **Calendar** in the navigation rail.

You see every calendar shared with the whole organization, plus those limited to one of your roles. You can add and change events on any calendar you can see. Creating, archiving and restricting calendars depend on your role.

- [Browse the calendar](browse-the-calendar.md): switch between day, week, month and agenda views, move through dates, and find an event.
- [Add an event](add-an-event.md): put a meeting, a moment or a full-day event on a calendar, with reminders.
- [Change or delete an event](change-or-delete-an-event.md): drag it to a new time, edit its details, or remove it.
- [Set up calendars](set-up-calendars.md): add a calendar, choose who sees it, archive or restore it.
- [Set your view preferences](set-your-view-preferences.md): hide weekends, pick the first day of the week, and more.
- [Import and export events](import-and-export-events.md): move events in from, or out to, another calendar app.
