# Set up calendars

Each calendar groups events under its own name and color, and can be limited to certain roles.

## Add a calendar

You need permission to manage calendars; without it, **Add calendar** isn't shown.

1. Under **My calendars** in the sidebar, click **Add calendar**.
2. Enter a **Name**. Optionally add a **Description** and pick a **Color**.
3. Under **Accessible to**, tick the roles that should see the calendar. Leave them all unticked to share it with everyone in your organization. The list shows the roles you hold.
4. Click **Save**.

## Edit a calendar

1. Click **More options** (**⋯**) next to the calendar and choose **Edit**.
2. Change its details and click **Save**. Changing **Accessible to** needs permission to manage calendars.

## Archive a calendar

You need permission to delete calendars.

1. Click **⋯** next to the calendar and choose **Archive**.
2. Click **Archive** to confirm. The calendar disappears from the list; its events are kept.

To restore it, open **Archived** at the bottom of the sidebar and click **Restore** next to it. Only people who can manage calendars see **Archived**.

Good to know: every organization has a **Personal** calendar, created automatically and shared with everyone. It can't be archived.
