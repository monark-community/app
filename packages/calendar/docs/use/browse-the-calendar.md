# Browse the calendar

## Switch view

1. Open the view menu in the toolbar. It shows the current view, for example **Week**.
2. Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Agenda**. **Agenda** lists upcoming events in order.

You can also press D, W, M or A.

## Move through dates

1. Click the arrows in the toolbar to go back or forward by a day, a week or a month, depending on the view. The arrow keys do the same.
2. To jump to a date, pick it in the small month calendar in the sidebar.
3. Click **Today**, or press T, to come back to the current date.

In Week and Month views, click a day's date to open that day in Day view.

## Show or hide a calendar

Click a calendar's name under **My calendars** in the sidebar. A hidden calendar is dimmed and its events disappear. Hiding isn't saved: the calendar shows again when you switch view or reload the page.

## Find an event

1. Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux), or click **Search** in the app bar.
2. Type part of the event's title or description. Matching events are listed under **Events**, with their date.
3. Select an event to open its day with the event selected.

## On a phone

The calendar shows Day view only. Tap the date at the top to pick another day, and use the row of calendars above the timeline to show or hide them.
