# Change or delete an event

## Move it or change its length

In Day or Week view:

1. Drag the event to a new time. In Week view you can also drag it to another day. To change only its end time, drag the handle on its bottom edge.
2. The **Edit event** form opens with the new time. Click **Save** to keep it.

Dragging works with a mouse and on a touch screen. In Month and Agenda views, open the event and change its date instead.

## Change its details

1. Click the event. The **Edit event** form opens.
2. Change any field, including its **Calendar** or **Type**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Delete it

1. Click the event.
2. Click **Delete**, then **Delete** again to confirm.

Good to know: your admin can connect a data model to the calendar, so records with a date appear as events. Change those through the record, not the event: the event follows the record's date, and disappears when the date is cleared or the record is deleted.
