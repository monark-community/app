# Find a page

## Browse the page tree

1. In the sidebar, click the arrow next to a page to show or hide its subpages. The sidebar remembers which branches you left open in this browser.
2. Click a page to open it. The breadcrumb above its title shows the pages it sits under; click one to go up a level.

When you open a page from a link or from search, the sidebar expands to show where it sits.

## Search titles and content

1. Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux), or click **Search** in the app bar.
2. Type a few words from the page's title or body. Matching pages are listed under **Pages**, and close misspellings still match.
3. Select a page to open it.

Search works from anywhere in the app, so you don't need to be in the wiki.
