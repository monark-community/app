# Create a page

Add a page at the top of the tree, or nest it under another page. You need permission to create pages.

## Create a top-level page

1. Click **New page** at the top of the sidebar. A page titled **Untitled** opens.
2. Replace **Untitled** with your title.
3. To give the page an icon, click the **＋** to the left of the title and type or paste an emoji.

The title and icon save as you type, and the sidebar updates to match.

## Create a subpage

1. In the sidebar, hover over the page you want to nest under and click its **+** (**Add subpage**). On a touch screen the button is always visible.
2. Give the new page a title and icon as above.

The parent page expands in the sidebar to show its new subpage, at the end of its list.
