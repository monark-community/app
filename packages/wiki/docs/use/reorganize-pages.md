# Reorganize pages

Each of these starts from the page's **Page actions** menu (**⋯**): hover over the page in the sidebar and click **⋯**. On a touch screen the button is always visible.

## Move a page to a new parent

You need permission to edit pages.

1. Open the page's **⋯** menu and choose **Move to…**.
2. Pick the new parent page, or **Top level** to put it at the root of the tree. The page itself and its own subpages aren't offered.
3. Click **Move**.

The page lands at the end of its new parent's list, and its subpages move with it.

## Duplicate a page

You need permission to create pages.

1. Open the page's **⋯** menu and choose **Duplicate**. The copy opens. It sits under the same parent as the original, at the end of the list.

Only the page itself is copied, not its subpages.

## Delete a page

You need permission to delete pages.

1. Open the page's **⋯** menu and choose **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm. The page and all of its subpages are deleted.

You can't bring a deleted page back from the wiki, so check the branch before you confirm.
