# Wiki

Keep your organization's shared notes as a tree of pages, nested as deep as you need. Everyone with wiki access sees the same pages. Open **Wiki** in the navigation rail; if it isn't there, the wiki isn't enabled for your organization, so ask your admin.

Viewing, creating, editing and deleting pages are separate permissions, set by your role. They apply to every page: if you can edit one page, you can edit them all. On a phone, the page tree is behind the **Pages** button at the top of the screen.

- [Create a page](create-a-page.md): add a page at the top level or under another page.
- [Write a page](write-a-page.md): add text, headings, lists and checklists, and rearrange them.
- [Reorganize pages](reorganize-pages.md): move a page to a new parent, copy it, or delete it.
- [Find a page](find-a-page.md): browse the page tree or search page titles and content.
