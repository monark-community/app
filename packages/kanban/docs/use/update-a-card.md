# Update a card

## Move it to another stage

Drag the card to another column. Or open the card, change its **Status**, and click **Save**.

If the column has a card limit and the move takes it over, the column header turns red. The move still goes through; the limit only warns.

## Reorder it within a column

Drag the card up or down. The order is shared, so everyone sees the same sequence.

## Change its details

1. Click the card to open it.
2. Change any field: assignees, reviewers, due date, priority, estimate, or the description and its checklist.
3. Click **Save**.

## Delete it

1. Open the card.
2. In its **Danger zone**, click **Delete** and confirm.
