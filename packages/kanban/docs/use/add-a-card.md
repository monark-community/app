# Add a card

1. Click **Add card** on the column the task belongs in.
2. Enter a **Title**. It's the only required field.
3. Fill in what you know:
   - **Assignees** and **Reviewers**: one or more people from your organization.
   - **Due date**, **Priority** (Low, Medium, High or Critical) and an **Estimate**.
   - **Description**: type `/` for headings, lists, quotes and more.
4. Click **Create**.

Assignees and reviewers appear as avatars on the card. Each person you add as an assignee is notified in the app, and by email if they've chosen to be.

![A board: one column per stage, cards showing assignees, priority, due date and checklist progress.](../../../../docs/assets/kanban-board.png)

## Add a checklist

In the description, type `/` and choose **Check List**, then add one item per line. The card shows a progress bar as items are ticked off.
