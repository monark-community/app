# Administer

For admins: set up your organization so everyone else can get to work. Admins only; if you hold the Administrator role, **Admin** appears in the navigation rail. Setting up a new organization? Start with [Set up your organization](../get-started/set-up-your-organization.md).

- [People and access](people-and-access/_index.md): bring people in, and decide what each can see and do.
- [Data models](data-models/_index.md): design your organization's databases, who can use them, and their public forms.
- [Integrations](integrations/_index.md): connect Monark to other systems with secrets, connected services, webhooks and service accounts.
- [Organization](organization/_index.md): organization-wide settings: name and color, files, achievements, audit trail.
