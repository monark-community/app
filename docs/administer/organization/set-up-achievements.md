# Set up achievements

Achievements are badges people earn automatically for things they do in the app, such as creating ten records or moving a card to Done. You define each badge and the conditions that award it. If Admin has no **Achievements** tab, achievements aren't enabled for your organization.

1. In **Admin**, open **Achievements** and click **New achievement**.
2. Enter a **Name**, a **Description** and the **Points** it's worth. Optionally upload an image for the badge.
3. Click **Save**.
4. On the new achievement, click **Add condition**.
5. Under **When this happens**, pick the event to count, such as a card being created.
6. In **Times**, enter how many of those events earn the badge.
7. Under **Award to**, pick who gets the credit, usually the person who did it.
8. To count only some of those events, click **Add filter** under **Only when (optional)** and pick a field and the value it must equal, such as the column a card moved into.
9. Click **Save**.

Counting starts now: things people did before the condition existed don't count. Each person's progress is their own, and they see it in their badge gallery; see [Achievements](../../use/achievements.md).

Good to know:

- An achievement with no conditions can't be earned. With several conditions, meeting any one of them awards it.
- Turn an achievement off with its **Enabled** switch to stop awarding it without deleting it.
