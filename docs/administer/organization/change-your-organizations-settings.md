# Change your organization's settings

Set the name, logo and brand color people see across the app, on the sign-in page and in the emails Monark sends.

1. In **Admin**, open **Organization**.
2. Click the logo area to upload a logo: a square JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 2 MB. It saves as soon as it's uploaded; **Replace** and **Remove** change it later.
3. Edit the **Display name**.
4. Enter a **Primary brand color** as a hex code, such as `#2563EB`. Leave it blank to use your deployment's default color.
5. Click **Save changes**.

The brand color becomes the accent color of the app for everyone in your organization, and of its emails.

## Name the AI assistant

If assistant naming is turned on for your organization, an **AI assistant name** field appears on the same page. Enter what the assistant should be called in chat and in its own replies, then click **Save changes**. Leave it blank to keep the default name, shown in the field.
