# Publish a public form

Let people without an account submit records to a model through a link, for a bug report, a survey or a feature request. Only the fields you pick are shown; the rest of the model stays private. If a model's editor has no **Public forms** section, public forms aren't enabled for your organization.

1. In **Admin**, open **Data Models** and click the model.
2. Under **Public forms**, click **New form** and enter a **Form name**.
3. Under **Who can submit**, choose **Anyone with the link** or **Invited people only**. A shared link is rate-limited; an invited person gets their own link and can submit once.
4. Under **Fields on the form**, tick the fields to ask for. Required fields must be included.
5. Optionally add an intro and a thank-you message.
6. Click **Create form**.

Share the form with **Copy link** on its card. For an invited-only form, enter each email address on the card and click **Invite**; each recipient shows as **Pending** or **Submitted**.

Submissions arrive as ordinary records; see [Triage records from a public form](../../use/data/triage-records-from-a-public-form.md).

## Pause or delete a form

Turn off **Active** on the form's card to stop taking submissions. **Delete form** kills its link and any invitations; records already submitted are kept.
