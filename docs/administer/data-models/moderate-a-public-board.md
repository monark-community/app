# Moderate a public board

A public board is a searchable, read-only list of a form's submissions, such as a feature-request board people can browse, vote on and discuss. Nothing appears on it until you approve it.

## Turn on a board

You switch the board on when you [create the form](publish-a-public-form.md):

1. Turn on **Public board**.
2. Under **Who can browse the board**, choose **Anyone with the link** or **Only invited people**.
3. Under **Fields shown publicly**, tick the fields visitors see, such as a status. The entry's title always shows.

## Approve or reject submissions

1. In **Admin**, open **Data Models** and click the model.
2. On the form's card, **Pending review** lists new submissions.
3. Click **Approve** to publish an entry on the board, or **Reject** to keep it off.

Either way, the submission stays in the model as a record.

## Votes and comments

Under **Engagement** in the model's editor:

- **Voting** lets signed-in users and invited people upvote entries, and lets the board sort by **Top**.
- **Discussions** lets signed-in users comment on entries. Comments appear immediately.

To moderate a comment, open the board while signed in. Admins see **Hide** and **Delete** on each comment. A hidden comment is shown to you with a **Hidden** badge and disappears for everyone else; **Unhide** restores it.
