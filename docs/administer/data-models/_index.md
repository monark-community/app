# Data models

Design your organization's databases: which models exist, what fields they hold, who can work in them, and how people outside the app can contribute. Your teammates then work with the records under **Data**; see [Data](../../use/data/_index.md).

Admins only. If you hold the Administrator role, **Admin** appears at the bottom of the navigation rail (in the menu on a phone); these pages start at its **Data Models** tab.

- [Create a data model](create-a-data-model.md): add a new database, or delete and restore one.
- [Add and change fields](add-and-change-fields.md): shape what every record holds.
- [Give a role access to a model's records](give-a-role-access-to-a-models-records.md): decide who can read, add, change and delete records.
- [Show a model's records on a calendar](show-a-models-records-on-a-calendar.md): turn each record into a calendar event.
- [Publish a public form](publish-a-public-form.md): let people without an account submit records through a link.
- [Moderate a public board](moderate-a-public-board.md): approve submissions for a public list and manage votes and comments.
