# Add and change fields

Fields are a model's columns: what every record holds, such as a status, a due date or a link to a client.

1. In **Admin**, open **Data Models** and click the model.
2. Under **Fields**, click **Add field**.
3. Enter a **Label** and pick a **Type**, such as **Text**, **Date**, **Single select**, **Relation** or **Formula**. You can't change the type later.
4. Fill in the options the type asks for, such as the choices of a select or the **Target** of a relation. Tick **Required** if every record must have a value.
5. Click **Save**.

The field appears in the record form and the column picker straight away.

## Change or reorder a field

Open the field's **…** menu and choose **Edit** to change its label, description, options or whether it's required. Drag a field by its handle to move it.

## Archive a field

Choose **Archive** from the field's menu and confirm. It disappears from forms and the column picker, but records keep their values. **Unarchive** brings it back. The title field can't be archived.

## Speed up filtering on a large model

Choose **Index this field** from the field's menu. The index builds in the background; the field shows **Indexed** when it's ready.
