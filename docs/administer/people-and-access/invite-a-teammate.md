# Invite a teammate

Send someone an email invitation to join your organization, with their role already chosen.

1. In **Admin**, open **Users** and click **Invite user**.
2. Enter their **Email**. Optionally add their **Full name**; the email greets them by it and it pre-fills their display name.
3. Pick a **Role**. You can change it later.
4. Click **Send invite**.

The invitation appears at the top of the user list with a **Pending** badge. The link in the email works for 14 days. When they sign up through it, they join with the role you picked and the row becomes a regular user.

## Cancel an invitation

In the user list, open the **…** menu on the pending invitation and choose **Revoke invite**. The link in the email stops working immediately.
