# People and access

Bring people into your organization and decide what each of them can see and do.

Admins only. If you hold the Administrator role, **Admin** appears at the bottom of the navigation rail (in the menu on a phone); these pages start there. Admin asks you to set up two-factor authentication first.

- [Invite a teammate](invite-a-teammate.md): send someone an invitation with their role already chosen.
- [Change someone's roles](change-someones-roles.md): give a person a role or take one away, including making them an admin.
- [Create a role](create-a-role.md): bundle the permissions a group of people needs.
- [Limit a role to some records](limit-a-role-to-some-records.md): let a role read only the records that match a query.
- [Manage a user's account](manage-a-users-account.md): fix a profile, send a password reset, adjust notifications.
- [Remove a user](remove-a-user.md): delete an account, with or without a grace period.
- [Set up two-factor for admin access](set-up-two-factor-for-admin-access.md): what the two-factor requirement asks of admins.
