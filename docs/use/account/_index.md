# Your account

Sign in, keep your account secure, and decide how Monark reaches you. To open your account settings, click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Profile**, **Account & Security** or **Notifications**.

- [Create an account](create-an-account.md): sign up with your email address.
- [Sign in](sign-in.md): get into the app with your email, a connected provider, or a two-factor code.
- [Reset your password](reset-your-password.md): get back in when you've forgotten it.
- [Edit your profile](edit-your-profile.md): set your name, picture, language and theme.
- [Read your notifications](read-your-notifications.md): see what happened and clear what you've dealt with.
- [Choose your email notifications](choose-your-email-notifications.md): decide which events also email you.
- [Turn on two-factor authentication](turn-on-two-factor-authentication.md): require a code from your phone at sign-in.
- [Change your email or password](change-your-email-or-password.md): update how you sign in.
- [Connect a sign-in provider](connect-a-sign-in-provider.md): sign in with an outside account as well as, or instead of, a password.
- [Sign out other devices](sign-out-other-devices.md): see where you're signed in and end sessions you don't recognise.
- [Create an API key](create-an-api-key.md): let a script or integration act as you.
- [Delete your account](delete-your-account.md): schedule your account for deletion, or cancel it.
