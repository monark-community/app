# Read your notifications

A number on your avatar at the top right counts your unread notifications.

1. Click your avatar. The **Notifications** tab opens, showing **Unread** items.
2. Click **Open** on a notification to go to what it's about. It's marked as read.
3. Use the **…** menu on a notification to **Mark as read**, **Mark as unread** or **Dismiss** it.

To clear everything at once, click **Mark all read**. To see notifications you've already read, switch to **All**. Click **Load more** at the bottom for older ones.

The count refreshes every 15 seconds and whenever you come back to the tab.
