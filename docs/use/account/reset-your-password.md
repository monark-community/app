# Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Click the link in the email, or type its 6-digit code and click **Continue**. Both are valid for one hour.
4. Enter a **New password**, confirm it, and click **Update password**.
5. If you use two-factor authentication, enter the current code from your authenticator app when asked.
6. Sign in with your new password.

Good to know:

- If an admin reset your password for you, you get the same email; start at step 3.
- The new password follows the same rules as when you [created your account](create-an-account.md).
- If you've lost your authenticator and your recovery codes, you can't finish the reset yourself. Ask your organization's administrator to recover your account.
