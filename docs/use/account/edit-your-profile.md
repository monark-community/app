# Edit your profile

Set how you appear to others and how the app looks for you.

1. Click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Profile**.
2. Change what you need:
   - **Avatar** and **Banner**: click either to upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image (up to 2 MB for the avatar, 5 MB for the banner), then drag and zoom to crop it. Click again to **Replace** or **Remove** it.
   - **Display name**: how your name appears in the app, up to 80 characters.
   - **Bio**: a short line about you.
   - **Language**: **English** or **Français**. Emails the app sends you follow it too.
   - **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom. Pictures are saved as soon as you crop them.

While your account is scheduled for deletion, your profile is read-only.
