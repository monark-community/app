# Connect a sign-in provider

Sign in with an outside account, such as Google or GitHub, as well as your password. The providers you see are the ones your organization offers; if there are none, this section doesn't apply.

1. Click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Account & Security**.
2. Under **Connected accounts**, click **Connect** next to the provider.
3. Approve the request on the provider's page. You come back to Monark with the provider marked **Connected**.

![Connected accounts: each provider with Connect or Disconnect, and email and password as one more way in.](../../assets/connected-accounts.png)

## Disconnect a provider

Click **Disconnect** next to it and confirm. You can reconnect it any time.

You can't disconnect your only way to sign in; it's labelled **Your only way in**. [Set a password](change-your-email-or-password.md#set-a-password-if-you-only-use-a-provider) or connect another provider first.
