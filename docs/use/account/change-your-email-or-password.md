# Change your email or password

Both live under your avatar at the top right: open the **Account** tab and choose **Account & Security**.

## Change your email

1. Under **Email**, click **Change email**.
2. Enter the **New email** and your **Current password**, then click **Send confirmation**. With two-factor on, enter your code when asked.
3. Confirm from both inboxes: in each email, click the link or type its 6-digit code into the dialog and click **Verify**. The dialog keeps your progress while you switch inboxes.
4. Once both sides confirm, you're signed out. Sign in with the new address.

If you can't reach the new inbox yet, start with the code from your current address; the dialog then asks for the other one.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, click **Change password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice.
3. Click **Update password**. With two-factor on, enter your code when asked.

You get an email confirming the change. The new password follows the same rules as when you [created your account](create-an-account.md).

## Set a password if you only use a provider

If you signed up with an outside provider, you have no password yet, and you need one before you can change your email.

1. Under **Password**, click **Set a password**.
2. Enter the **New password** twice and click **Set password**.
