# Delete your account

Deleting is scheduled, not instant: your account is held for 14 days, and you can cancel any time before then.

1. Click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, choose **Profile**, then open the **Danger zone** tab.
2. Click **Delete account** and read what deletion does.
3. Type your email address to confirm, then click **Delete my account**.

You get an email with the date the deletion completes. Until then, the app only opens your read-only profile and the **Danger zone** tab.

When the 14 days are up, your memberships and roles are removed. What you contributed, such as votes and comments, stays but is anonymized.

## Cancel the deletion

1. Sign in. You land on the **Danger zone** tab.
2. Click **Cancel deletion**. Your account works as before.
