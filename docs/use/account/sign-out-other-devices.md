# Sign out other devices

See every browser and device signed in to your account, and end the ones you don't recognise.

1. Click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Account & Security**.
2. Under **Trusted devices**, find the device. Each shows its browser and system, when it was first and last seen, and roughly where. The one you're using is marked **This device**.
3. Click **Revoke** on it. That device is signed out at once.

If you think your account is compromised, click **Revoke all** and confirm with **Revoke everything**. Every device is signed out, including this one, and you sign in again from scratch. Then [change your password](change-your-email-or-password.md).

## Choose how long a device stays trusted

With two-factor on, a device that has passed the code check skips it on later sign-ins until its trust runs out. Under **Trust window**, pick **30 days**, **60 days** or **90 days**. The window restarts at each successful sign-in.

Good to know:

- An email about a sign-in from a new device has a link to revoke that device straight away, without signing in.
