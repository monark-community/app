# Create an account

1. On the sign-in page, click **Create one**.
2. Enter your **Email** and a **Password**. The strength meter rates the password as you type.
3. Optionally enter a **Display name**: the name people see next to your avatar. You can add it later.
4. Click **Create account**. A confirmation email is on its way.
5. Click the link in the email, or type the 6-digit code from it on the **Check your email** page and click **Verify**. You're signed in.

If your organization offers sign-in with an outside provider, you can click **Continue with** that provider instead and skip the password.

Good to know:

- A password needs at least 12 characters and three kinds of character (lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols). It can't contain your email or display name, or be one that has appeared in a known data breach.
- No email? Click **Resend email**. If you typed the wrong address, click **Sign up again**.
