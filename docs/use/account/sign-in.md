# Sign in

1. Open the sign-in page. If your organization offers outside providers, click **Continue with** one of them and you're done.
2. Otherwise enter your **Email** and click **Continue**. If the address shown is wrong, click **Change**.
3. Enter your **Password** and click **Sign in**. You land on the home page.

![The first sign-in step: provider buttons, then the email field and Continue.](../../assets/signin-step-one.png)

## Enter a two-factor code

If you've [turned on two-factor authentication](turn-on-two-factor-authentication.md), a code page appears after your password on a device you haven't used before.

1. Enter the 6-digit **Code** from your authenticator app.
2. Click **Verify**.

Lost your phone? Click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of the codes you saved. Each works once.

Good to know:

- A banner on the sign-in page tells you why you're there: **Email updated** or **Password updated** means sign in with the new details.
- If your account is scheduled for deletion, you land on the **Danger zone** tab so you can [cancel it](delete-your-account.md).
- If you're an admin without two-factor and your 7-day grace period has run out, every page except your account sends you back to set it up.
