# Choose your email notifications

Decide which events also send you an email. In-app notifications are managed for you; email is the part you choose.

1. Click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Notifications**.
2. Find the event under its category (**Security**, **Account**, **Activity**) or type in **Search notifications…**.
3. Tick it to get an email, or clear it to stop. Each change saves straight away.

To switch a whole category, use the checkbox on its heading. To undo all your choices, click **Reset to defaults**.

Good to know:

- Events marked **Required** are security alerts, such as a sign-in from a new device. They protect your account, so their emails can't be turned off.
- Only events that can send email are listed.
