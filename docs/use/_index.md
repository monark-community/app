# Use Monark

How to get your everyday work done in the app, one page per task. New here? [Take your first steps](../get-started/first-steps.md). You only see the areas your organization uses; if one is missing from your navigation, ask your admin.

- [Your account](account/_index.md): sign in, keep your account secure, and decide how Monark reaches you.
- [Search and navigation](search-and-navigation/_index.md): get to an area, or straight to a record, card or page by name.
- [Data](data/_index.md): find, add and keep track of records in your organization's databases.
- [Kanban](../../packages/kanban/docs/use/_index.md): plan and track work as cards on a board.
- [Calendar](../../packages/calendar/docs/use/_index.md): plan events on shared, color-coded calendars.
- [Wiki](../../packages/wiki/docs/use/_index.md): write and organize your organization's shared pages.
- [Automations](automations/_index.md): draw flows that run by themselves when something happens.
- [Assistant](assistant/_index.md): ask questions about your data and approve the changes it makes.
- [See your achievements](achievements.md): the badges you've earned and the ones still to earn.
