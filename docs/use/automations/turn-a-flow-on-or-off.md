# Turn a flow on or off

A flow runs by itself from its trigger only while it's on. New flows start off.

1. Open the flow.
2. Switch on **Enabled** in the top bar. Switch it off to pause the flow; you can still test it with **Run now**.

From the list of automations, you can do the same without opening the flow: open the **…** menu on its row and choose **Enable** or **Disable**. The **Status** column shows **Active** or **Disabled**.

## Delete a flow

Open the **…** menu on its row, choose **Delete**, and confirm. This can't be undone.
