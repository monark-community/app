# Search and navigation

Get to the part of the app you need, or straight to a record, card or page by name. You only see the areas your organization has turned on and your role can reach.

- [Move around the app](move-around-the-app.md): use the navigation rail, the phone menu, the breadcrumb and section tabs.
- [Search across the app](search-across-the-app.md): jump to any record, card, event, page or automation by name.

Every shortcut is listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).
