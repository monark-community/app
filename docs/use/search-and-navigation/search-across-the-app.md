# Search across the app

Jump to a record, card, calendar event, wiki page or automation by name, or to any area of the app.

1. Click the magnifier at the top right, or press ⌘ K (Ctrl K on Windows and Linux).
2. Type at least two characters. Results appear grouped as **Records**, **Cards**, **Events**, **Pages** and **Automations**, with matching areas under **Go to**.
3. Click a result, or move to it with the arrow keys and press Enter.

Press Esc to close search without going anywhere.

Good to know:

- You only find things you're allowed to see, in areas turned on for your organization.
- Some areas also have their own search box for filtering what's on screen, such as a model's records or a board's cards.
