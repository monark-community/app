# Move around the app

Each area of the app (**Calendar**, **Kanban**, **Wiki**, **Automation**, **Data**) has an icon in the navigation rail down the left edge. Hover an icon to see its name; the area you're in is highlighted.

![The navigation rail: the logo at the top, one icon per area with the current one highlighted, and the admin shield at the bottom.](../../assets/navigation-rail.png)

- **Go home**: click the logo at the top of the rail.
- **Open an area**: click its icon.
- **Go back up a level**: click a link in the breadcrumb at the top of the page.
- **Switch between an area's pages**: use the tabs beside the content, such as the models in **Data** or the tabs in your account. On a narrow screen they become a strip across the top.
- **Reach admin settings**: click the shield at the bottom of the rail. It's only there if you're an admin.

## On a phone

1. Tap the logo at the top left. A menu slides in with **Home**, every area, and the admin button if you have it.
2. Tap where you want to go. Close the menu with the X or by tapping outside it.

Good to know:

- The top right of every page holds the assistant (where it's turned on), search, and your avatar, which opens your notifications and account.
- Every page has its own address, so you can bookmark it or send the link to a colleague.
