# Restrict who can see a record

Hide a single record from everyone except the roles you choose, for example a salary record or a confidential deal. You need permission to manage the model's records.

1. Open the record.
2. In its **Access** section, select the roles that should see it.
3. Click **Save access**.

The record disappears, not even listed, for anyone without one of those roles. Admins always see every record.

To open it back up, clear every role and click **Save access**. A record with no roles selected is visible to everyone who can access the model.
