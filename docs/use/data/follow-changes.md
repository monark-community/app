# Follow changes

Get an in-app notification when something you care about changes.

## Follow one record

1. Open the record.
2. Click **Follow**. The button changes to **Following**.

You're notified when the record changes or is deleted. Click **Following** to stop.

## Follow a whole model

1. Open the model.
2. Click **Follow** in the toolbar above the table. On a phone, open the **…** menu and turn on **Follow this list**.

You're notified whenever any record in the model is created, changed or deleted.

Good to know:

- You're never notified about your own edits, or about records you can't see.
- To get these by email as well, [choose your email notifications](../account/choose-your-email-notifications.md). In-app notifications are always on.
