# Data

Your organization keeps its own databases in Monark, such as Projects, Clients or Feature requests. Each one is a **model**, and each row in it is a **record**. Your admins decide which models exist and what fields they have; open **Data** in the navigation rail to see the ones you can access.

What you can do in a model depends on your role. If a button on these pages is missing for you, your role doesn't include that permission.

- [Find records](find-records.md): search, filter and page through a model.
- [Save a view](save-a-view.md): keep a filter you use often, for yourself or your team.
- [Create a record](create-a-record.md): add a row to a model.
- [Edit or delete a record](edit-or-delete-a-record.md): change a record, remove it, or bring it back.
- [Edit many records at once](edit-many-records-at-once.md): set one field across a batch of records.
- [Follow changes](follow-changes.md): get notified when a record or a whole model changes.
- [Restrict who can see a record](restrict-who-can-see-a-record.md): hide one record from everyone outside chosen roles.
- [Triage records from a public form](triage-records-from-a-public-form.md): complete records that people outside the app submitted.
