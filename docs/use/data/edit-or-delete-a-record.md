# Edit or delete a record

## Edit

1. Click the record in the table. It opens in a panel beside the list. To give it the whole screen, choose **Open full page**.
2. Change any field.
3. Click **Save**. **Cancel** closes the record without saving.

Formula fields update on their own when you change the fields they depend on.

## Delete

1. In the table, open the record's **…** menu and choose **Delete**. You can also delete from inside the open record.
2. Confirm.

## Restore a deleted record

Deleted records can be brought back until they're permanently removed.

1. In **Filters**, set **Status** to **Include deleted**.
2. Open the deleted record's **…** menu and choose **Restore**.
