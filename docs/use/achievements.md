# See your achievements

Achievements are badges your organization awards automatically as you work, such as for creating records or moving cards. You don't apply for them. If there's no **Achievements** card in your account menu, achievements aren't turned on for your organization.

1. Click your avatar at the top right and open the **Account** tab. The **Achievements** card shows how many badges you've earned and your latest ones.
2. Click the card to open the full gallery. The count at the top reads, for example, "4 of 11 earned".
3. Read each badge:
   - Badges you've earned are in full colour, with their points and the date you earned them.
   - Badges you haven't earned are greyed out. Once you've started on one, a progress bar shows how far you've got, such as 3 / 5; until then it reads **Locked**.

Good to know:

- Progress is yours alone; a teammate's work doesn't count toward your badges.
- Only work done after an admin set up a badge counts toward it.
- Points are a label on each badge, not a balance you spend.
- A badge that never moves may have no conditions yet. Admins set badges up; see [Administer](../administer/_index.md).
