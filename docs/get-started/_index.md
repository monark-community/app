# Get started

Pick the path that matches why you're here. Each is a single guided lesson that ends with something working.

- [Take your first steps](first-steps.md): sign in, find your way around, add a record and follow it. For everyone who uses the app.
- [Set up your organization](set-up-your-organization.md): secure your admin access, create a first database and role, and invite a teammate. For the first admin.
- [Set up a development environment](set-up-a-development-environment.md): run Monark on your machine and make a first change. For developers.
- [Deploy your first instance](deploy-your-first-instance.md): put a staging copy of Monark online and sign in as its admin. For operators.
