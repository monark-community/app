# The assistant

Monark has a built-in AI assistant that lives one tap away and can both answer
questions about your data and make changes for you — always with your say-so.

> The assistant only appears when your organization has turned it on. If you
> don't see the ✨ icon, it isn't enabled for you yet.

## Opening it

- Click the **✨ icon** in the top bar (it's there on every page), or
- Press **Ctrl / ⌘ + J**.

A panel slides in from the right. It stays out of the way — you can keep clicking
around the app while it's open. On a phone it opens full-screen; close it with the
X or the back arrow.

## Conversations

The first screen lists your conversations. Tap **New chat** (the ➕ in the panel
header) to start a fresh one, or pick an existing conversation to continue it. Use
the back arrow to return to the list.

## Chatting

Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** (Shift+Enter for a new line).
The assistant knows which page you're on, so you can say things like "add a task
here" or "what's in this list?" and it understands the context.

It can:

- **Answer questions** about your data — "how many open records are in Projects?"
- **Take actions** — "create a task called Follow up", "update this record's
  status to Done".

## Confirming changes

The assistant will **never change anything without asking**. When it wants to
create, update, or delete something, it shows a small card describing the action
with **Confirm** and **Reject** buttons. Nothing happens until you press Confirm.
Read-only lookups (just reading your data) run on their own.

You're always in control, and the assistant can only ever see or change what
**you** are allowed to — it acts with your own permissions, nothing more.

## Good to know

- Replies stream in as they're written (after a brief "Thinking…" moment).
- Your conversations are private to you.
- If the assistant says it can't do something, it's usually because you don't have
  permission for that action, or the data isn't there.
